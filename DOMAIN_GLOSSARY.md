*   **Confidence-Fidelity Divergence Index (CFDI)**: A metric defining the gap between how confident an agent's generative text appears and how structurally verifiable the underlying claim is. A CFDI > 0.15 triggers Epistemic Escrow.
*   **Semantic Saponification Index (SSI)**: A measure of the frequency of RLHF-homogenized, information-null phrasing detected in an output. Must remain below 0.04.
*   **Semantic Density Score (SDS)**: A metric measuring the information bits per token by penalizing filler and hedge words. Axiom targets an SDS > 0.85.
*   **Polyglot Hallucination Resonance (PHR)**: An AI failure mode where an agent fills an epistemic gap with the most statistically probable pattern from its training corpus rather than the system's actual reality.

Eradicated by enforcing structural, hardware-grounded optical parameters.
