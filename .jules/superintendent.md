- **Instability**: Loose dependency versions (`^`, `~`) in `package.json` created risk of non-deterministic builds and unintended minor/patch regressions pulling into CI/CD pipelines. Legacy `.eslintrc.json` coexisted with `eslint.config.mjs`, creating hallucination risk for linter resolution.
- **Fortification**: Swept legacy `.eslintrc.json` to enforce singular flat config source of truth. Executed strict dependency pinning by resolving all `^` and `~` semantics to exact versions recorded in `package-lock.json` to enforce reproducible and immutable infrastructure builds without crossing major version boundaries.
